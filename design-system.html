Overview

Brand Promise

Cool — an unbroken cold chain with traceability and food-safety as table stakes.

Ready — product arrives prepped and operationally reliable.

Who We Serve

Restaurants, grocery retailers, and food distributors who rely on consistent, fresh seafood delivery.

Design Approach

Clean, cool, and confident. Modern cold-storage facility meets a good fishmonger's counter.

Brand Guidelines

Voice & Tone

Direct and reassuring. Short, declarative sentences. Lead with customer outcomes backed by proof.

Example: "One unbroken cold chain — from the dock to your back door."

Color Rule

Harbor teal does the heavy lifting. Tide aqua for accents and links. Marine blue is sparse (~5% of surface).

Imagery

Cool-leaning, fresh seafood and cold-chain photography. Crisp focus, natural light, slightly cool white balance.

Iconography

System: Lucide via CDN. Stroke style, 20–24px in UI, always paired with text labels.

Typography Scale

Display: Space Grotesk (headlines)

Body: IBM Plex Sans (UI + text)

Data: IBM Plex Mono (SKUs, lots, temps)

Surfaces

Default page: Ice-50 (#F2F8F9)

Cards: White with 1px border

Dark sections: Harbor-900
